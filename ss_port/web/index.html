How to play

Click the game, then press Space or click PLAY. ← → change lane, ↑ jumps, ↓ rolls. On a phone, swipe.

Crashing ends the run as usual. So does a coin: the first one you touch stops you dead. Magnets are a trap, and so is anything that pulls coins toward you.

A bot plays this challenge too, for over an hour: watch its runs.
