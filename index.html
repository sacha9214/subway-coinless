How to play

Click the game, then press Space or click PLAY. ← → change lane, ↑ jumps, ↓ rolls, F goes full screen. On a phone, swipe.

Crashing ends the run as usual. So does a coin: the first one you touch stops you dead. Magnets are a trap. You get one life: there is no revive. After a run, Space plays again and H goes back home.

Sign in with Google to put your time on the leaderboard. Only the name you choose is shown. Each saved run keeps its track and its key presses, so it can be played back.

A bot plays this challenge too, for over an hour: watch its runs.
